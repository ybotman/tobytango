# Tango History Research Task: Tango & Paris — Two Waves

You are a tango history researcher. Your task is to write a comprehensive white paper covering **tango's two transformative encounters with Paris**: the 1912-1914 Tangomania and the 1983 "Tango Argentino" premiere that sparked the global revival.

## Eras Being Researched

- **Era 1:** First Tangomania (1910-1914)
- **Era 2:** Paris Explosion / Tango Argentino (1983-1985)
- **Category:** europe
- **Theme:** Paris as the city that twice validated tango for the world

## What We Already Know

### First Tangomania (from guardia-vieja.md)
- Tango reaches Paris ~1907-1913 via wealthy Argentine travelers
- 1913 called "the year of the tango"
- Pope Pius X condemns tango (November 1913)
- Kaiser Wilhelm II bans officers from dancing it (November 17, 1913)
- Tango transforms women's fashion (shorter dresses, no corsets)
- Canyengue sanitized — cortes y quebradas smoothed out
- Ricardo Güiraldes, Enrique Saborido among early introducers

### Tango Argentino Show (from decadencia.md, ballroom-era.md)
- Claudio Segovia & Héctor Orezzoli, creators
- Paris premiere: Théâtre du Châtelet, November 11, 1983
- Broadway: Mark Hellinger Theatre, October 9, 1985 (198 performances)
- Cast included: Copes & María Nieves, Virulazo & Elvira, Gloria & Rodolfo Dinzel
- Cast flew to Paris on military cargo plane
- Rescued forgotten milongueros from obscurity

### Gap: The 70 Years Between (1914-1983)
- What happened to tango in Paris during interwar period?
- How did it survive WWII?
- What thread of authenticity persisted that made Paris the right place for revival?
