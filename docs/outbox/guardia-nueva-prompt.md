## RESEARCH DIRECTIVE

Create a comprehensive white paper documenting **La Guardia Nueva (The New Guard, 1920–1935)** — the era when tango transformed from rough street music into the sophisticated art form that would dominate the Golden Age.

**WHY THIS MATTERS:** This is the bridge era. Everything that makes Golden Age tango what it is — the orquesta típica format, the bandoneon's dominance, the sophisticated arrangements, the cantor integration — was invented during La Guardia Nueva. Julio De Caro's 1924 revolution is the single most important musical development between tango's birth and its peak. Carlos Gardel became the global voice of tango and died at its end, marking the transition to the Golden Age.

Without understanding La Guardia Nueva, you can't understand why the Golden Age sounded the way it did.
