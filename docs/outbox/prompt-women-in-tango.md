# Research Request: Women in Tango (Thematic Paper + Timeline)

## Purpose
Creating a thematic paper for tobytango.com covering women's often-erased history in tango — from forbidden participation to modern leadership. This is NOT a full timeline category with era bands, but a dedicated page with:
1. Narrative white paper
2. Timeline bullets (key moments/figures)
3. Cross-references to other categories

## Why Thematic (Not Category)

Women's story in tango cross-cuts ALL eras rather than following its own linear progression. A woman singing in 1940 and a woman teaching in 2020 aren't in different "women's eras" — they're in different tango eras with gendered experiences. The thematic approach lets us surface this dimension across existing categories.

### Key Topics to Cover

**I. The Early Prohibition**
- Why were "respectable" women forbidden from tango?
- The arrabal, prostitution, and moral panic
- Men dancing with men as norm
- When did this change? What enabled it?

**II. The Milonguitas**
- Working-class women who danced
- Literary/musical representation (Linning's "Milonguita" 1920)
- Were they celebrated or condemned?

**III. Female Orchestras**
- Did they exist? When?
- Orquesta de Señoritas?
- Why so rare? What happened to them?

**IV. The Great Female Singers**
- **Azucena Maizani** (1902–1970)
  - "La Ñata Gaucha"
  - Cross-dressed, sang "male" tangos

- **Mercedes Simone** (1904–1990)
  - "La Dama del Tango"

- **Libertad Lamarque** (1908–2000)
  - "La Novia de América"
  - Film star, exiled by Peronismo

- **Tita Merello** (1904–2002)
  - "Se Dice de Mí"
  - Actress, singer, cultural icon

- **Nelly Omar** (1911–2013)
  - 102 years old at death
  - Peronista, banned during dictatorship

- **Amelita Baltar** (b. 1940)
  - Piazzolla's voice for nuevo tango

- **Adriana Varela** (b. 1952)
  - "La Gata" — modern era star

- **Susana Rinaldi** (b. 1935)
  - International ambassador of tango song

**V. Women in the Golden Age Milongas**
- What was the experience of women dancing in 1940s milongas?
- The codes: cabeceo, seating, comportment
- Agency or constraint?

**VI. The Show Era: Female Stage Dancers**
- María Nieves (Copes' partner)
- Milena Plebs (Zotto's partner)
- Were women partners or artists in their own right?

**VII. The Teaching Revolution**
- Women as tango teachers in global spread
- Breaking the leader/follower = man/woman equation
- Key female teachers:
  - Susana Miller
  - Graciela González
  - Nora Dinzelbacher
  - Others?

**VIII. Queer Tango & Role Fluidity**
- When did queer tango emerge?
- Women leading
- The challenge to traditional codes
- Key figures and communities

**IX. Women Leading Today**
- Female DJs (musicalizadoras)
- Female organizers
- Contemporary female performers
- Has equality arrived?

## Research Questions

1. **The prohibition**: Exactly when/how were women excluded? When did this change?

2. **Milonguitas**: Real social phenomenon or literary trope?

3. **Female orchestras**: Any documented examples? Why the erasure?

4. **Singer agency**: Did female singers choose their repertoire? Write lyrics?

5. **María Nieves**: Her perspective on the partnership with Copes? Her own artistry?

6. **Teaching shift**: When did women become prominent teachers? Who pioneered?

7. **Queer tango origins**: First queer milongas? Key founders?

8. **Current state**: What barriers remain? What's changed?
