## RESEARCH DIRECTIVE

Document the revival of the orquesta típica format from 1990 to present — the new generation of tango orchestras that emerged after the near-extinction of live tango music during La Decadencia.

**WHY THIS MATTERS:** For 35 years (1955–1990), the orquesta típica was nearly dead. The Golden Age recordings survived, but live orchestras playing danceable tango had almost vanished. Starting around 1990, a new generation began rebuilding — first by preserving (Color Tango playing Pugliese arrangements), then by innovating (Fernández Fierro's punk energy), and eventually by training new musicians (Escuela de Tango). This is the story of tango's musical resurrection.
