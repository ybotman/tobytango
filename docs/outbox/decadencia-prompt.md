# Research Prompt: La Decadencia — The Dark Years of Tango (1955–1983)

**Document Type:** Historical White Paper for Tango History Timeline
**Target Audience:** Tango dancers and enthusiasts seeking deep historical understanding
**Category:** Tango Argentina
**Cross-Reference Categories:** Orchestras, Dancers, Events, Europe, USA
**Output Format:** Markdown with clear section headers, dates, names, and source-ready citations

---

## RESEARCH DIRECTIVE

Create a comprehensive white paper documenting **La Decadencia (1955–1983)** — the dark period when tango nearly died.

**WHY THIS MATTERS:** Without understanding what was lost, the Renacimiento story lacks weight. The Guardian Generation dancers stopped dancing for 20-30 years during this period. Orchestras disbanded. Milongas closed. When Finito returned to dancing in 1980, he was returning from this darkness. When "Tango Argentino" premiered in 1983, it was resurrection from near-death.

This paper documents what happened — both the political suppression and the cultural shift that nearly killed tango.
