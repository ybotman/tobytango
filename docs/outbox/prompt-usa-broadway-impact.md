# Tango History Research Task: Broadway Impact & American Tango Revival

You are a tango history researcher. Your task is to write a comprehensive white paper on the **American tango revival (1985-1995)** sparked by "Tango Argentino" on Broadway.

## Era Being Researched

- **Era Name:** Broadway Impact
- **Category:** usa
- **Years:** 1985–1995
- **Predecessor:** ballroom-era.md (covers 1910-1985)

## What We Already Know (from ballroom-era.md)

- "Tango Argentino" opened at Mark Hellinger Theatre, October 9, 1985
- 198 performances, Tony nomination
- Americans saw real Argentine tango for first time
- Daniel Trenner: "Johnny Appleseed of tango" — first BA tours, 80+ videos
- Orlando Paiva: One of first authentic teachers in USA (LA, early 1970s)
- Complete disconnect before 1985: Americans knew only ballroom tango

   - How long did it run? Tours?

2. **First Teachers & Communities (1985-1990)**
   - Who were the first to teach authentic Argentine tango in USA?

   - Where did they learn? Direct from Argentina or from show cast?
   - Early venues, practica spaces

3. **The Pilgrimage Begins (1987-1995)**
   - When did Americans start traveling to Buenos Aires?
   - Daniel Trenner's role — first organized tours?
   - What did they find when they got there? (Renacimiento era)
   - Who did they study with? (Guardian Generation)

4. **Community Formation (1990-1995)**
   - First milongas in major cities
   - Teaching methodologies — what did early teachers emphasize?
   - Open embrace vs. close embrace debates
   - Instructional videos (Trenner's 80+)

5. **Key Figures**
   - Daniel Trenner — detailed profile
   - Rebecca Shulman
   - Paul Pellicoro (NYC)
   - Nora Dinzelbacher (NYC)
   - Jorge Dispari & María del Carmen (early visitors)
   - Others who seeded American communities
