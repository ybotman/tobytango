# Research Prompt: La Investigación — The Tango Investigation Era (1995–2005)

**Document Type:** Historical White Paper for Tango History Timeline
**Target Audience:** Tango dancers seeking to understand the analytical movement
**Category:** Argentina
**Cross-Reference Categories:** Dancers (Nuevo Innovators), Europe, USA
**Output Format:** Markdown with clear section headers

---

## RESEARCH DIRECTIVE

Create a comprehensive white paper documenting **the Investigation Era (1995–2005)** — the decade when a group of young Argentine dancers systematically analyzed tango's movement vocabulary and created an analytical framework that would reshape how tango is taught worldwide.

**WHY THIS MATTERS:** This era represents tango's intellectual awakening. Where the Guardians danced instinctively and the Bridge Generation codified styles, the Investigators asked "WHY does tango work?" They reduced all movement to fundamental principles, created new vocabulary, and opened tango to dancers from other disciplines. This is also the most misunderstood era — "tango nuevo" is widely misused as a style label when the investigators explicitly reject this.

### II. THE ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK

1. **The Three Fundamental Steps**
   - Cruce adelante (front cross)
   - Apertura (open/side step)
   - Cruce atrás (back cross)
   - How does everything reduce to these three?

2. **The Turn Code (Código de Giro)**
   - Apertura → cruce atrás → apertura → cruce adelante
   - How this explains all turns
   - Alteraciones and cambios de dirección

3. **Parallel/Crossed System**
   - Evolution of terminology: "even/uneven" → "parallel/crossed" → "normal/crossed"
   - Why this vocabulary matters

4. **Axis Analysis**
   - On-axis vs. off-axis work
   - Development of volcadas (inward-falling)
   - Development of colgadas (outward-hanging)
   - Soltadas (disconnected)

5. **Leading from the Center**
   - Torso-based leading
   - Influence of Contact Improvisation
   - Why embrace style becomes "irrelevant to movement structure"

### III. THE "NUEVO" QUESTION

1. **What They Actually Said**
   - Naveira's 2009 essay: "Tango Nuevo is everything that has happened with tango since the 1980s. It is not a question of a style."
   - Chicho: "To think that 'Tango Nuevo' is something that occurred only 10 years ago is a commercial exploitation"
   - Why they reject the style label

2. **What IS New**
   - Systematic off-axis work (volcadas, colgadas)
   - The analytical methodology itself
   - Open embrace as valid option (not requirement)

3. **What Is NOT New**
   - The movements themselves — derived from Golden Age milongueros
   - Close embrace — investigators use it too
   - Musicality — different interpretation, same music
