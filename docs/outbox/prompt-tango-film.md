### Key Topics to Cover

**The Gardel Films (1931–1935)**
- Paramount's French and Spanish productions
- Which films? How many?
- "El Día Que Me Quieras," "Cuesta Abajo," etc.
- Impact: brought Gardel (and tango) to global Spanish-speaking audience
- The tragedy: all collaborators died at Medellín

**Early Argentine Tango Cinema (1930s–1950s)**
- ¡Tango! (1933) — Luis Moglia Barth, first sound film
- Libertad Lamarque's films
- Tita Merello's films
- The Golden Age of Argentine cinema and tango
- Which films captured authentic milonga culture?

**The Dark Period (1955–1980s)**
- Did tango appear in film during Decadencia?
- Military dictatorship and cinema
- Tangos: el exilio de Gardel (Solanas, 1985)

**The Saura Era (1990s–2000s)**
- Carlos Saura's "Tango" (1998) — Oscar nominated
- Visual innovation, choreography, artifice
- Impact on international perception

**The Sally Potter Moment (1997)**
- "The Tango Lesson" — semi-autobiographical
- Pablo Verón as lead
- Showed the LEARNING process, not just performance
- Impact on non-dancers' perception

**Commercial Tango (1990s–2000s)**
- "Scent of a Woman" (1992) — Al Pacino's tango (Pellicoro trained him)
- "Assassination Tango" (2002) — Robert Duvall
- "Take the Lead" (2006)
- Other Hollywood tangos

**Documentaries**
- "Café de los Maestros" (2008) — surviving Golden Age musicians
- "Our Last Tango" (2015) — Copes & María Nieves
- "Si Sos Brujo" (2005)
- "Yo No Sé Qué Me Han Hecho Tus Ojos" (2003)
- Other significant documentaries?

**The Forever Tango Phenomenon**
- Luis Bravo's show (1990 SF → 1997 Broadway)
- Gavito's impact
- Show as gateway drug

**Modern / Streaming Era**
- Any significant recent tango films?
- YouTube and the democratization of tango footage
- Has film been replaced by social media?
