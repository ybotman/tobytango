# Research Prompt: From Nuevo Peak to Neo-Traditional (2005–Present)

**Document Type:** Historical White Paper for Tango History Timeline
**Target Audience:** Tango dancers understanding contemporary evolution
**Category:** Argentina
**Cross-Reference Categories:** Dancers, Europe, USA, Events
**Output Format:** Markdown with clear section headers

---

## RESEARCH DIRECTIVE

Create a comprehensive white paper documenting **the arc from nuevo's peak visibility (2005–2008) through the neo-traditional turn (2008–present)** — the period when tango's pendulum swung from open embrace experimentation back toward Golden Age values, culminating in the encuentro movement and a renewed focus on close embrace, musicality, and códigos.

**WHY THIS MATTERS:** This is the tango that most current dancers learned. Understanding this arc explains the style debates, the festival circuit, the marathon/encuentro format, and the current state of global tango communities.
