- **Slug:** chicho-frumboli
- **Type:** dancer

## What We Already Know

From existing papers on tobytango.com:

### Basic Facts
- **Born:** September 21, 1970, in Buenos Aires
- **Background:** Rock drummer for 14 years, studied theater with Cristina Banegas
- **Conversion to tango:** 1993, in a class with Ricardo Barrios and Victoria Vieyra
- **Arrived in Europe:** 1998

### Career
- Victoria Vieyra introduced him to Naveira/Salas's investigation group
- Naveira said: "With only six months of study, he was amazing as a tango dancer"
- Naveira also said: "Chicho was absolutely the best dancer I ever saw"
- Between 1994-1998, studied with Barrios/Vieyra and Tete Rusconi
- Spent much of the 2000s in Paris expanding tango vocabulary
- Performed live with Gotan Project, Tanghetto, and Narcotango

### Partners (timeline)
- Victoria Vieyra (early)
- Lucía Mazer (1999–2003)
- Eugenia Parrilla (2003–2006)
- Juana Sepúlveda (2007–present)

### Style
- Known as "the master of the rebound"
- Pioneered 50/50 partnership dynamic rather than strict lead-follow
- Created new sacadas
- Embraced swing rhythms rooted in rock-and-roll

### Key Quotes (already have)
- "I was into rock-and-roll; I had long hair and played the drums. I hated tango, I didn't like it one bit. But when I went to take a class with Ricardo Barrios and Victoria Vieyra, I embraced my dance partner for the first time and I got goose bumps."
- "Tango Nuevo does exist, but it has for a very long time. Copes was dancing a new tango, Miguel Angel Zotto had a new tango. Every once in a while there is someone who appears and proposes something new."
- "To think that 'Tango Nuevo' is something that occurred only 10 years ago is a commercial exploitation that we owe to the festival organizers." (March 2008)

### The El Tangauta Interview (December 2009)
His famous self-critique to Milena Plebs:
> "The problem is that we missed something in the teaching, I take total responsibility. I can't pass on what I have learned. I was crazy about creating, because I saw a new vein in the evolution of the movement. I threw myself into that, and I lost the way to be able to pass on the tango essence."
