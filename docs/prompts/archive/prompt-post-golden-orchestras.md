# Research Prompt: Post-Golden Age Orchestras — The Decadencia from a Musical Perspective (1955–1990)

**Document Type:** Historical White Paper for Tango History Timeline
**Target Audience:** Tango dancers seeking to understand the musical decline and survival
**Category:** Orchestras
**Cross-Reference Categories:** Argentina (Decadencia), Events
**Output Format:** Markdown with clear section headers

---

## RESEARCH DIRECTIVE

Create a comprehensive white paper documenting **the post-Golden Age orchestral era (1955–1990)** — the 35-year period when orquestas típicas collapsed from hundreds to single digits, the Big Four died one by one, and tango music survived only through stubborn individuals, reduced ensembles, and one remarkable orchestra that refused to stop.

**WHY THIS MATTERS:** While the Decadencia killed milongas and scattered dancers, it devastated the music industry even more thoroughly. Understanding what happened to the orchestras explains why today's milongas still play 1940s recordings — because so little danceable music was produced for three decades.
