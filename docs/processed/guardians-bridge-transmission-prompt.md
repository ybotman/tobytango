**Document Type:** Historical White Paper for Tango History Timeline
**Target Audience:** Tango dancers and enthusiasts seeking deep historical understanding
**Category:** Dancers & Couples
**Cross-Reference Categories:** Argentina (Renacimiento), Events, Europe, USA
**Output Format:** Markdown with clear section headers, dates, names, and source-ready citations

## RESEARCH DIRECTIVE

Create a comprehensive white paper documenting **the transmission of tango knowledge from the Guardian Generation to the Bridge Generation and Stage Pioneers (1980–2010)** — the critical 30-year window when tango was passed from the bodies of aging milongueros to a new generation who would codify, globalize, and preserve it.

**WHY THIS MATTERS:** This is the story of how tango survived. The Guardians carried Golden Age tango in their bodies through 20-30 years of Decadencia silence. When they returned to the floor in the 1980s, they became involuntary research subjects, teachers, and cultural treasures. The Bridge Generation sat at their feet, learned, codified what they saw into teachable methodology, and took it worldwide. The Stage Pioneers brought it to Broadway and international theaters. Without this transmission, tango would have died with the last milonguero.

This paper documents WHO taught WHOM, HOW the knowledge was codified, and WHERE it spread.
