# Carolina del Rivero

From ballet to tango nuevo. Carolina del Rivero brings classical training from Teatro Colón and Teatro San Martín to her work as one of tango's premier teachers and performers. Fabián Salas' primary partner through the 2000s, she was a master teacher at CITA for 14 consecutive years (2001–2014). A certified Feldenkrais practitioner since 2020, she now combines somatic awareness with tango instruction worldwide.

---

## Biography

### Classical Foundation

Carolina started dancing at age 6 and trained in multiple techniques throughout her career. She studied ballet at the world-famous **Teatro Colón** — the most renowned opera house in Latin America — and later modern dance at the **Taller de Danza Contemporánea del Teatro San Martín**.

### Discovering Tango (1995)

In 1995, Carolina discovered tango. Her classical training gave her exceptional body awareness and technical precision, which she brought to the emerging nuevo movement.

### Partnership with Fabián Salas

Carolina became Fabián Salas' main partner, teaching and performing together at major festivals worldwide. Their partnership represented the nuevo aesthetic — analytical, technically demanding, and visually dramatic.

They appeared in the **Best of Cosmo Tango** DVDs (2003, 2004, 2005, 2006), documenting the heights of the nuevo era.

### International Teaching Career (2000–)

Since 2000, Carolina has traveled the world giving workshops and exhibitions in the USA, Europe, Latin America, Central America, and Asia.

### CITA Master Teacher (2001–2014)

Carolina was a master teacher and dancer at the **Congreso Internacional de Tango Argentino (CITA)** — the world's largest annual tango event in Buenos Aires — for 14 consecutive years.

### Later Partnerships

After her partnership with Salas, Carolina has performed and taught with **Donato Juárez** and **Ricardo Biggeri**.

### Feldenkrais Practitioner (2020)

Carolina studied the Feldenkrais Method — a technique of awareness through movement that develops efficient, effortless body use. She graduated as a certified Feldenkrais Practitioner in 2020 and now combines Feldenkrais with tango in seminars in Argentina and worldwide.

## Teaching Style

### Classical Meets Tango

Carolina's training in ballet and contemporary dance gives her teaching a strong technical foundation. She emphasizes body awareness, alignment, and efficient movement.

### Feldenkrais Integration

Her Feldenkrais work focuses on awareness through movement — understanding how to use the body without excess effort. This complements tango's demand for connection and subtle communication.
