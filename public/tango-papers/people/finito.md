# Finito (Ramón Rivera)

Elegance personified. Ramón "Finito" Rivera was considered the best social dancer of the 1980s — the personification of elegance in tango. After stopping dancing for 20-25 years, he returned to the milongas in the early 1980s revival and became one of the most highly regarded dancers of Tango Estilo del Barrio. His dancing was smooth, precise, creative, passionate, and deeply connected to the music. He died in 1987 at approximately 55 years old.

---

## Biography

### Early Dancing

Finito was among the legendary milongueros who had danced through tango's Golden Age, absorbing the styles of the 1940s and 1950s.

### Hiatus from Tango

Like many milongueros, Finito stopped dancing tango for 20-25 years — roughly from the early 1960s through the early 1980s — as tango faded during the *Decadencia* period.

### Return to Dancing (Early 1980s)

When the tango revival began in the early 1980s, Finito returned to the milongas. He was considered the best social dancer of the decade.

### Death (1987)

Finito died in 1987 at approximately 55 years old, before the full globalization of tango but after helping to spark the revival.

---

## Style

### Tango Estilo del Barrio

Finito was one of the most highly regarded dancers of **Tango Estilo del Barrio** in the 1950s who was still dancing in the 1980s.

### Classic Villa Urquiza Style

He demonstrated the classic Villa Urquiza style, as documented in Ney Melo's "Origins of Villa Urquiza Style" video series.

### Characteristics

His dancing was characterized as:
- **Smooth** — Flowing, continuous movement
- **Precise** — Exact execution
- **Creative** — Improvised vocabulary
- **Passionate** — Emotional connection
- **Musical** — Deeply connected to the music

### Elegance

He was called "the personification of elegance in tango."

## Legacy

Finito represents the generation of dancers who:
- Learned tango in its Golden Age
- Lived through the decline (*Decadencia*)
- Returned to dance during the revival
- Passed knowledge to the next generation before they died
