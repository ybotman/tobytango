# Carlitos Espinoza

The Chilean milonguero. Carlos "Carlitos" Espinoza brought pure milonguero style from Chile to the world stage. He began teaching in Argentina and Chile in 1998, reached Europe in 2001, and by 2004 was instructing full-time. His partnership with [Noelia Hurtado](/tango-papers/people/noelia-hurtado) (2011–) became legendary for their fierce, sensual connection — powerful and confident meets sultry and elegant.

---

## Biography

### Chilean Origins

Born March 8, 1981 in Chile, Carlitos developed his milonguero-based style outside Argentina's capital.

### Teaching Career Begins (1998)

At just 17, Carlitos began teaching in Argentina and Chile — an unusually young start for a tango teacher.

### European Career (2001)

In 2001, Carlitos reached Europe. Three years later, the young milonguero-style dancer found himself instructing full-time (2004).

### Partnership with Noelia (2011–)

In 2011, Carlitos began working with Noelia Hurtado. Their partnership became legendary for their unmistakable connection — something "beyond compare."

## Style

### Milonguero Base

Carlitos' dance style is based on tango milonguero — close embrace, musical, connected.

### Powerful and Confident

In his partnership with Noelia, Carlitos was the powerful, firm, confident presence — complementing her sultry elegance.

### Fierce and Sensual

Together they used their characteristics to create "a fierce and sensual tango" with connection that "overflowed in their performances."
