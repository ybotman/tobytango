# Ignacio Varchausky

The most important preservation figure in contemporary tango. Ignacio Varchausky founded Orquesta El Arranque (1996), created the Orquesta Escuela de Tango Emilio Balcarce to train young musicians, won a Latin Grammy as producer, and founded TangoVia Buenos Aires to preserve and spread tango culture worldwide. With El Arranque, he has performed in 150+ cities across four continents, including Lincoln Center with Wynton Marsalis. He is ensuring that tango's orchestral tradition survives into the next generation.

---

## Biography

### Double Bass Player

Varchausky is a double bass player — carrying forward the tradition of the *contrabajo* in tango orchestras.

### Founding Orquesta El Arranque (1996)

In 1996, Varchausky and bandoneonist Camilo Ferrero founded Orquesta El Arranque as a quintet in Buenos Aires. The orchestra developed a repertoire readily embraced by the tango community — performing over 200 shows in their first year alone.

### Building a Repertoire

El Arranque focused on authentic tango arrangements, connecting the contemporary scene to Golden Age traditions.

### Creating Orquesta Escuela (Training the Next Generation)

Varchausky is the creator and artistic director of **Orquesta Escuela de Tango Emilio Balcarce** — an orchestra-school for young tango musicians in Buenos Aires.

The documentary *Si sos brujo: A Tango Story* documents the birth of this school, featuring Varchausky alongside maestro Emilio Balcarce.

### TangoVia Buenos Aires

Varchausky founded **TangoVia Buenos Aires**, a non-profit organization aiming to preserve, spread, and develop tango culture throughout the world.
