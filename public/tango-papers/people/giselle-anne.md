# Giselle Anne

The other half of nuevo's mind. Giselle Anne has been Gustavo Naveira's dance and life partner since approximately 1995 — instrumental in the conceptualization and development of tango nuevo. Classically trained from age three, she learned from the legendary milongueros Antonio Todaro and Pepito Avellaneda before tango schools existed. Together with Naveira, she co-created a comprehensive tango methodology focused on deep analysis of tango movement and structure. Based in Boulder, Colorado, they run the Boulder Tango Festival and continue to shape tango pedagogy worldwide.

---

## Biography

### Classical Foundation (Age 3+)

Giselle Anne began dancing at three years old with formal ballet education. Her academic dance training included contemporary dance, ballet, and folklore for more than 10 years.

### Self-Taught Tango (1980s)

She pursued tango largely self-taught in the 1980s, when tango instructors were scarce. This independent study preceded the formalization of tango pedagogy.

### Learning from the Masters

She had the rare opportunity to learn from two of the most renowned milongueros:
- **Antonio Todaro** — One of the most influential teachers of the revival
- **Pepito Avellaneda** — Guardian generation legend

### Early Career: International Tours

Early in her career, Giselle Anne collaborated with prestigious artists:
- Libertad Lamarque
- Argentino Ledesma
- Mariano Mores
- José Collangelo
- Los Solistas de D'Arienzo
- Dragone
- Tripodi

She toured extensively across Europe, America, and Asia, and performed in Buenos Aires' tango houses and theaters.

### Meeting Gustavo Naveira (~1995)

Giselle Anne met Gustavo Naveira in Spain, at Sitges, dancing together in a show. Their partnership began around 1995 and continues to this day.

### Co-Creating Nuevo Methodology

Together with Naveira, she co-created "a comprehensive tango methodology focused on a deep analysis of tango movement and structure." While Naveira is renowned for conceptualizing nuevo tango, Giselle Anne was instrumental in exploring and developing the concept.

### Boulder, Colorado

Gustavo and Giselle Anne now live in Boulder, Colorado, where they:
- Operate their own tango studio
- Teach tango seminars
- Run the **Boulder Tango Festival** (since 2010, September/October annually)

## Teaching Methodology

### Intellectual Approach

Giselle Anne and Naveira are known for their analytical, intellectual approach to teaching — focusing on understanding the structure and mechanics of tango movement.

### Comprehensive Training

Her teaching covers:
- Fundamental movements (enrosques, barridas, boleos)
- Rhythm patterns
- Balance and coordination
- Terminology
- Choreography
- Fitness

### Accessibility

Classes accommodate all skill levels for both leaders and followers.

## Boulder Tango Festival (BTF)

Since 2010, Gustavo and Giselle Anne organize the Boulder Tango Festival each September/October in Colorado — bringing international tango to the American West.
