# Ricardo Vidort

The philosopher milonguero. Ricardo Vidort (1929–2006) was a great dancer with a philosopher's mind and a degree in parapsychology. He believed tango was not merely a series of steps but a profound expression of life's joys and sorrows, conveyed through the intimate embrace. One of the few milongueros of his generation to travel internationally, he brought authentic Buenos Aires tango to Europe and the US in his final years.

---

## Biography

### Buenos Aires Roots

Born in 1929 in Buenos Aires, Ricardo Vidort was a lifelong tango dancer. He grew up during tango's Golden Age, absorbing the authentic milonga culture.

### Mentor: Carlos Estévez (Petroleo)

One of his most significant relationships was with his mentor Carlos Estévez ("Petroleo"). This mentorship during Vidort's formative years instilled a deep appreciation for the artistic and emotional aspects of tango, shaping his approach with emphasis on feeling and expression over technical proficiency.

### Education

Highly educated, Vidort held a degree in parapsychology. This intellectual background informed his philosophical approach to tango.

### International Teaching

Vidort was one of the few milongueros of his generation to travel internationally. In his later years, he went to Europe and the US to teach, sharing authentic Buenos Aires tango with the global community.

### Death

He died of cancer on May 21, 2006, in Santa Fe, New Mexico — far from Buenos Aires but surrounded by the tango community he had touched.

## Philosophy

### Tango as Expression

Vidort believed that tango was not merely a series of steps but a profound expression of life's joys and sorrows, conveyed through the intimate embrace of dance.

### Feeling Over Technique

His mentor Petroleo shaped his approach: emphasis on feeling and expression over mere technical proficiency.

### Teaching with Empathy

His teaching philosophy was grounded in empathy and understanding, making his classes not only informative but inspiring.

### Love and Generosity

He was a man of true heart who built his life around tango and his friends. His love for humanity, unselfishness, and generosity were his light.
