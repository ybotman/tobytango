# Alfredo De Angelis

The orchestra of the people. Alfredo De Angelis created tango that was "easily understood by the people" — honest, traditional, and perfect for dancing. While lacking the artistic transcendence of Troilo or Pugliese, he compensated with accessibility and joy. His happy, irresistible waltzes and pioneering vocal duos — especially the legendary Dante and Martel — made him a milonga favorite for decades. With 486 recordings on Odeon, his music remains essential for dancers.

---

## Biography

### Early Prodigy

Born November 2, 1910 in Adrogué, De Angelis showed profound musical inclination from an early age. He began studying piano, harmony, and sight-reading before turning five.

### Professional Debut (Age 15)

At fifteen, De Angelis began his professional career as pianist accompanying singer Juan Giliberti. He later joined Anselmo Aieta's orchestra, replacing pianist Juan Polito.

### Forming His Orchestra (1940-1941)

By 1940, De Angelis felt ready to create his own musical expression. On March 20, 1941, his orchestra debuted at the iconic Buenos Aires café Marzotto — a triumphant entry that quickly led to a recording contract with Odeon Records.

### Recording Career (1941-1977)

De Angelis remained with Odeon for 36 years, producing an astonishing 486 recordings. His consistent output made him one of the most recorded orchestras of the era.

## Musical Style

### Accessible Traditional

De Angelis sought refuge in traditional tango that was easily understood by the people. His honest approach made his music accessible to all dancers.

### Happy and Irresistible

His music is notably happy — particularly his early waltzes, which are "completely irresistible" for dancers.

### Vocal Duos

In the 1940s, De Angelis pioneered vocal duos in tango. His best numbers featured the harmony of these pairings, especially the famous duo of **Dante and Martel** — perhaps the best duo of the entire tango era.

### Perfect for Dancing

His music is described as "perfect for dancing" — clear rhythm, joyful energy, and easy to follow.

## For Dancers

De Angelis is beloved by dancers who enjoy:
- Happy, uplifting music
- Clear, danceable rhythms
- Beautiful waltzes
- Vocal harmonies

His accessible style makes him ideal for social dancing and for dancers who prefer joy over drama.
