# Silvia Ceriani

Tete's teaching partner. A writer and artist who became [Tete Rusconi's](/tango-papers/people/tete-rusconi) partner in 1995, Silvia Ceriani helped bring the authentic milonguero style to the world. Together they were known for the musicality and flowing creativity of their dancing — particularly their beautiful vals. Within a few short years, they were teaching around the globe, creating instructional videos on salon tango fundamentals and advanced waltz techniques.

---

## Biography

### Writer and Artist

Before her tango career, Silvia Ceriani was a writer and visual artist, bringing an artistic sensibility to her dance work.

### Partnership with Tete (1995)

In 1995, Silvia became Tete Rusconi's teaching partner. Their collaboration would span 15 years until Tete's death in 2010.

### International Teaching

Within a few short years of their partnership, Tete and Silvia were teaching around the world. They were particularly noted for their beautiful vals.

### Bringing Milonguero Style to the US

Tete was one of the first to teach close embrace style in the United States around 1994, under the auspices of Susana Miller. Silvia joined him in this mission, helping spread the authentic Golden Age salon style internationally.

### Instructional Materials

The pair created instructional videos on:
- Salon tango fundamentals
- Advanced waltz rhythm techniques

## Dance Style

### Musicality

Tete and Silvia were known for the musicality of their dancing — a hallmark of the milonguero tradition.

### Flowing Creativity

Their style featured flowing creativity within the structure of close embrace salon tango.

### Vals Specialization

They were particularly noted for their beautiful vals (tango waltz), capturing the romantic, swirling quality of the form.
