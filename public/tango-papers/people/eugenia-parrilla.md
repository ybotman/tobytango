# Eugenia Parrilla

Chicho's partner in the nuevo explosion. Eugenia Parrilla moved to Paris in 2001 and began working with Mariano "Chicho" Frúmboli, launching an international career of performances and teaching at festivals worldwide. Their partnership (2003–2006) captured the height of the nuevo movement. Since 2010, she has partnered with Yanick Wyler, deepening her work in traditional tango while maintaining expressive freedom.

---

## Biography

### Move to Paris (2001)

In 2001, Eugenia moved to Paris, which became her base for international tango work.

### Partnership with Chicho (2003–2006)

Eugenia began working with Mariano "Chicho" Frúmboli, and her international career took off with performances and teaching at festivals and workshops around the world. Their partnership showcased the nuevo aesthetic at its peak.

### Other Collaborations

She also worked with Ezequiel Farfaro and Pablo Inza, continuing to develop her artistry with different partners.

### Partnership with Yanick Wyler (2010–)

Since 2010, Eugenia has been teaming up with Yanick Wyler — her partner in life and dance. Together they have deepened their work in traditional tango while expressing themselves freely within the dance.

---

## Teaching Philosophy

Eugenia's approach emphasizes enjoying every part of the movement — finding pleasure in the process rather than just the result.

> "Enjoy every part of the movement."
