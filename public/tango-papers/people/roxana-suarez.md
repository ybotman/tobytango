# Roxana Suárez

Born into tango. Roxana Suárez started listening to tango music in her mother's womb — her mother attended milongas organized by grandmother Martita in the Congreso neighborhood. She grew up surrounded by tango, watching the best dancers and learning from a young age at Buenos Aires milongas. With [Sebastián Achaval](/tango-papers/people/sebastian-achaval) since 2007, she represents authentic milonga tango as one of the most recognized pairs in the world.

---

## Biography

### Family Heritage

Roxana's grandmother Martita organized milongas in the Congreso neighborhood of Buenos Aires. Her mother attended these milongas while pregnant with Roxana — so she literally began hearing tango music before birth.

### Growing Up in the Milongas

Roxana grew up surrounded by tango, watching the best dancers and learning from a young age in Buenos Aires milongas.

### Partnership with Sebastián (2007–)

In February 2007, Roxana began working with Sebastián Achaval. Since then, they have taught group and private lessons at well-known Buenos Aires venues and milongas, regularly performing on the city's most famous dance floors.

### International Recognition

Together with Sebastián, Roxana is recognized as one of the most eminent representatives of the young tango-de-salon generation, combining tradition with youthful energy.
