# Dimitris Bronowski

The curious tanguero. Dimitris Bronowski is the author of *Tangofulness* — the most translated tango book in the world (12 languages). A social dancer since 2009, he quit his job in 2019 to dedicate himself to tango full-time. His vision: help one million people experience moments of meaning through tango. Through his blog, podcast, and newsletter "The Curious Tanguero," he explores connection, awareness, and the philosophy of tango.

---

## Biography

### Beginning Tango (2009)

Bronowski began dancing tango in 2009. It has since enriched his life with "moments of meaning, friendships, love, family, happiness, travels, and over 3,000 prolonged hugs."

### Going Full-Time (2019)

In 2019, he quit his job to dedicate himself to his passion: tango.

### The Curious Tanguero

He is the creator of:
- **The Curious Tanguero** podcast
- **The Curious Tanguero** newsletter (TheCuriousTanguero.com)
- Tango blog
