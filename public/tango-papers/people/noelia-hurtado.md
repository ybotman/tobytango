# Noelia Hurtado

The Queen of Embellishments. Noelia Hurtado started her artistic career at the famous Club Sunderland in 2003, studied intensively with Villa Urquiza masters Carlos Pérez and Rosa Forte, and in 2006 became metropolitan champion of tango salon and sub-champion of milonga. Known for adding her personal touch to Villa Urquiza style while merging it with contemporary techniques, she became "the Queen of embellishments." Her partnership with [Carlitos Espinoza](/tango-papers/people/carlitos-espinoza) (2011–) was legendary for its fierce, sensual connection.

---

## Biography

### Club Sunderland Debut (2003)

Noelia started her artistic career at the famous Club Sunderland in 2003 — one of Buenos Aires' historic tango venues.

### Villa Urquiza Training

She studied intensively the well-known Villa Urquiza style with maestros Carlos Pérez and Rosa Forte, among other important teachers.

### Metropolitan Championships (2006)

In 2006, Noelia was selected as:
- Metropolitan Champion of Tango Salon of Buenos Aires
- Metropolitan Sub-Champion of Milonga

### Developing Her Style

Noelia went on to add her own personal touch to her dance, merging the attributes of Villa Urquiza tango and milonga with new elements and techniques of contemporary tango.

### "Queen of Embellishments"

Her distinctive use of adornos (embellishments) earned her the title "Queen of embellishments."

### Partnership with Carlitos (2011–)

In 2011, Noelia began working with Carlitos Espinoza. Their connection "overflowed in their performances" — something "beyond compare." Noelia was sultry, dramatic, and elegant, complementing Carlitos' power and confidence.

## Style

### Villa Urquiza Foundation

Her training with Carlos Pérez and Rosa Forte gave her a solid Villa Urquiza base — elegant, grounded, musical.

She merged Villa Urquiza attributes with new elements and techniques of contemporary tango, creating her distinctive style.
