# Milena Plebs

The dancer who writes. Milena Plebs brought classical training from Teatro San Martín's contemporary dance group to tango, then co-created Tango x 2 with Miguel Ángel Zotto — revolutionizing the concept of the tango show. A María Ruanova Award winner, Honorary Academic of Argentina's National Tango Academy, and longtime El Tangauta columnist, she bridges performance and scholarship. She conducted the famous 2009 El Tangauta interview with Chicho Frúmboli that documented the nuevo movement. Her life is dedicated to "investigating the origin, history and evolution of the tango dance."

---

## Biography

### Buenos Aires Roots

Milena Plebs was born and raised in the heart of Buenos Aires, the cradle of tango.

### Contemporary Dance Training (1980–1986)

Before tango, Plebs was a dancer with the prestigious Grupo de Danza Contemporánea del Teatro San Martín de Buenos Aires (1980–1986). This classical training in contemporary dance gave her "a unique seal" when she later brought it to tango interpretation.

### Tango Argentino (1986–1989)

Her career in tango began with participation in *Tango Argentino* by Claudio Segovia and Héctor Orezzoli, alongside Miguel Ángel Zotto (1986–1989). The show sparked the worldwide tango renaissance.

### Training with Antonio Todaro

Like Zotto, Plebs underwent rigorous training with the legendary Antonio Todaro — one of the most influential teachers of the revival era.

### Creating Tango x 2 (1990)

In the late 80s and 90s, Plebs co-founded **Tango x 2** with Zotto. The company "revolutionized the concept of tango show." Their innovative productions toured Europe, USA, and Asia.

## Journalism & Research

### El Tangauta

For many years, Plebs worked at El Tangauta magazine as a tango columnist. She conducted significant interviews, including the famous 2009 interview with Chicho Frúmboli that documented the tango nuevo movement.

### Research Focus

Plebs dedicates herself to "investigating the origin, history and evolution of the tango dance" — making her both practitioner and scholar.

### Dance Criticism

She continues publishing dance criticism alongside her performing and teaching work.
