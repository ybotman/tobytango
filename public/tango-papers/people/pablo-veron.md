# Pablo Verón

The feet of God. Pablo Verón is the tango dancer who brought the form to global cinema, starring opposite Sally Potter in *The Tango Lesson* (1997) and working with Johnny Depp, Robert Duvall, and Kirsten Dunst. The New York Times declared he "has the feet of God." Paris's Libération called him "the best dancer in the world." A Guggenheim Fellow and American Choreography Award winner, he bridges virtuosity with deep traditional roots, combining technical brilliance with the intuitive transmission of the old milongueros.

---

## Biography

### Early Career (1986)

Verón started his professional career in 1986 in Buenos Aires, performing in musical comedies including *Evita*, *Cabaret*, and *Sweet Charity*, as well as various tap and tango revues.

### His Own Company

He formed his own dance company in Buenos Aires while continuing to perform in major productions.

### Tango Argentino (1989)

Pablo first came to Paris in the acclaimed *Tango Argentino* in 1989 — the show produced by Segovia and Orezzioli that sparked the worldwide tango renaissance. The show went on to enjoy a sold-out world tour. This marked a turning point, establishing his international reputation.

### Paris Success (1991–)

In 1991, Verón began teaching tango and moved to Paris, where he choreographed and performed in numerous theatre productions. He performed in *Mortadella* by director Alfredo Arias, which won the Prix Molière for Best Musical in 1993.

### The Tango Lesson (1997)

His breakthrough to mainstream cinema came with Sally Potter's semi-autobiographical film *The Tango Lesson*, where he served as lead actor, dancer, and choreographer. The film won the Argentine Cinema Festival that year. Potter, blocked while writing her next film, abandoned the page to learn tango — instructed by Verón. The resulting film documents their artistic and romantic collaboration.

### Major Film Career

Following *The Tango Lesson*, Verón appeared in significant films:

- **The Man Who Cried** (2000) — with Johnny Depp
- **Assassination Tango** (2002) — with Robert Duvall
- **Upside Down** (2012) — with Kirsten Dunst
- **Our Last Tango** (2015) — documentary on Copes and Nieves

### Continuing Career

In more than 30 years of career, Pablo has developed an advanced methodology of teaching. He shares his passion and experience through intensive workshops and seminars in major cities worldwide.

## Philosophy

### "The Tango Chose Me"

Verón's relationship with tango has an almost mystical quality. As he states: "The tango chose me."

### On Personal Style

> "Before there were more personal styles because there was less commerce, less manipulation. There was a more intuitive system of transmission, more direct and honest."

### Teaching Methodology

Over 30+ years, Verón developed an advanced teaching methodology that bridges virtuosity with traditional roots. He combines:
- Technical brilliance and precision
- Character and expression
- Traditional aesthetics with contemporary development

## Artistic Characteristics

As described by critics, Verón combines:

- **Virtuosity** — Technical mastery of the highest order
