# Guido Iacopetti

The new tango sound. Guido Iacopetti founded Sexteto Fantasma in 2012, blending traditional tango with jazz, rock, and Latin American rhythms. His 2024 album *La inevitable tentación de ir a contramano* received a 2025 Latin Grammy nomination for Best Tango Album, and his composition "La vida del brujo" was nominated for the 2025 Gardel Prize as Best Tango Song. A composer, guitarist, arranger, and producer — he represents tango's contemporary evolution.

---

## Biography

### Founding Sexteto Fantasma (2012)

Iacopetti founded and directs **Sexteto Fantasma**, a new tango group that combines traditional tango elements with jazz, rock, and Latin American rhythm influences.

### Multi-Faceted Artist

He is:
- Author and lyricist
- Composer
- Guitarist
- Arranger
- Music producer
