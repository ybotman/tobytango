# Juana Sepúlveda

Power and subtlety. Juana Sepúlveda has been Chicho Frúmboli's dance partner since 2007, forming one of tango nuevo's most celebrated partnerships. With over 17 years of tango experience, she is described as "one of the youngest-born stars in the tango sky," combining "innovative subtlety and a stunning manifestation of power in every movement" with "unswerving musicality and pure sensuality." Together with Chicho, she tours the world teaching and performing at major festivals.

---

## Biography

### Partnership with Chicho (2007–present)

Juana and Chicho have been dance partners since 2007. Together they form a unique duo both on stage and in their teaching — recognized as one of tango nuevo's most dynamic partnerships.

### International Teaching

Since 2009, Chicho and Juana have taught and performed at the annual Tango Element Festival in Baltimore, United States. They conduct seminars and workshops at festivals and major schools worldwide.

### Style and Artistry

Juana is celebrated for:
- **Technique** — Over 17 years of tango experience
- **Musicality** — "Unswerving musicality"
- **Range** — "Spans the entire range of expression"
- **Innovation** — "Innovative subtlety"
- **Power** — "Stunning manifestation of power in every movement"
- **Sensuality** — "Pure sensuality"

### Role Model Status

Today Juana is considered "one of the role models as a tango dancer" — inspiring a new generation of followers worldwide.
