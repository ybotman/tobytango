# Sebastián Achaval

World Champion turned neotrad leader. Sebastián Achaval discovered tango at 15 when a friend enrolled him in classes, left Neuquén for Buenos Aires to pursue a professional career, and in 2005 became Tango de Salón World Champion with Ximena Gallicchio. Since 2007, his partnership with [Roxana Suárez](/tango-papers/people/roxana-suarez) has made them "one of the most recognized tango pairs in the world" — representing authentic milonga tango with youthful energy and genuine passion.

---

## Biography

### Patagonian Origins

Born and raised in Neuquén in Patagonia, Sebastián was far from Buenos Aires' milonga culture.

### Discovery at 15

A friend enrolled him in a tango school when he was 15. This chance introduction sparked a lifelong dedication to the dance.

### Move to Buenos Aires

Sebastián relocated to Buenos Aires to pursue tango professionally, immersing himself in the capital's milonga scene.

### World Championship (2005)

In 2005, Sebastián competed in the Tango World Championship and won the Tango de Salón category with partner Ximena Gallicchio.

### Partnership with Roxana (2007–)

In February 2007, Sebastián began working with Roxana Suárez. Since then, they have taught group and private lessons at well-known Buenos Aires venues and milongas, regularly performing on the city's most famous dance floors.
