# Steve Darmo

The endings expert. Steve Darmo listened to 1,700 tango songs and classified 9 common ending types — creating the first systematic study of how tangos end and how to respond with style. His book *Tango Endings* gives dancers the musicality tools to anticipate and dance song endings with confidence. Three years of research, distilled into a practical guide.

---

## Biography

### Research Journey

Darmo conducted research on tango endings for three years. He initially focused on steps and movements, but eventually realized that focusing on musicality instead of steps would lead to more fruitful discoveries.

### The 1,700 Tangos

He listened to 1,700 tango songs from Golden Age orchestras including:
- Aníbal Troilo
- Osvaldo Pugliese
- Juan D'Arienzo
- And many others

### Classification System

During his research, he classified:
- **9 common tango endings**
- **3 uncommon endings**

---

## Book: Tango Endings

**Full Title**: *Tango Endings: Learn the 9 Common Argentine Tango Song Ending Types, How to Anticipate Them, and 50 Musical Steps to Dance Them*

The first book to systematically treat the different types of tango endings and how to respond to them with style.

---

## Philosophy

### Musicality First

The key insight: focus on musicality instead of steps. Understanding how songs end — through listening — leads to better dancing than memorizing step patterns.

### Why Endings Matter

The ending of a tango is a moment of heightened attention. Dancing it well creates a lasting impression.
