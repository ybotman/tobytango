# Roberto Álvarez

Pugliese's heir. Roberto Álvarez was Osvaldo Pugliese's first bandoneonist from 1984 until the maestro's death, writing 25 orchestral arrangements for Pugliese's repertory. In 1989, he founded Color Tango to continue Pugliese's dramatic, powerful style. For over 30 years, Color Tango toured the world — USA, Japan, China, Europe, Latin America — keeping Pugliese's tradition alive for new generations of dancers. He died March 5, 2023, at age 82.

---

## Biography

### With Osvaldo Pugliese (1978–1995)

In 1978, Álvarez was invited by Osvaldo Pugliese to join the bandoneon section of his orchestra. From 1984, he served as lead bandoneonist, writing 25 orchestral arrangements for Pugliese's repertory.

### Founding Color Tango (1989)

In 1989, Álvarez founded **Color Tango Orchestra**. The orchestra debuted in Holland in January 1990.

### The Pugliese Style

He organized Color Tango's repertory to incorporate Pugliese's style into each arrangement he wrote — creating a direct continuation of the maestro's dramatic sound.

### Global Tours

Color Tango toured:
- USA
- Japan
- China
- Europe
- Central and South America

### Death (2023)

Roberto Álvarez died on March 5, 2023, at age 82.

A tribute concert was held at Pista Urbana on March 11, 2023, with the orchestra performing great classics and new arrangements.
