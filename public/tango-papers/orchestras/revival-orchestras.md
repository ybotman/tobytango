# Revival Orchestras: The New Golden Age (1990–present)

**After 35 years of near-silence, young Argentine musicians began rebuilding the orquesta típica tradition in the 1990s.** Today, dozens of professional orchestras perform in Buenos Aires milongas and tour internationally—from faithful Golden Age recreations to punk-tango cooperatives to fusion ensembles blending tango with jazz and world music.

---

## The Revival Story

### Seeds of Renewal (1980s–1990s)

The 1983 premiere of *Tango Argentino* in Paris triggered a global dance revival, but the music scene took longer to recover. By the late 1980s, surviving Golden Age masters like Leopoldo Federico, Horacio Salgán, and Osvaldo Pugliese were performing for increasingly enthusiastic audiences.

The key institutional development came in 2000 when bassist **Ignacio Varchausky** founded the **Orquesta Escuela de Tango Emilio Balcarce**. This school-orchestra brought together aging Golden Age masters with young musicians, creating a pipeline that has trained ~300 musicians who now populate the entire Buenos Aires tango scene.

### The Contemporary Landscape (2000–present)

Today Buenos Aires hosts a thriving ecosystem:

- **Neo-traditional** ensembles recreating Golden Age sounds (Color Tango, Tango Bardo)
- **Innovative milonguero** orchestras rearranging classics (Andariega, Romantica Milonguera)
- **Punk/alternative** groups pushing boundaries (OTFF, Astillero)
- **Fusion** ensembles blending genres (Sexteto Fantasma)
- **International** orchestras proving tango is now world music (Bandonegro, Hyperion Ensemble)

## Key Orchestras

### Color Tango (1990–present)

**Style:** Neo-traditional / Pugliese lineage

The primary successor of the Pugliesian tradition. Founded by **Roberto Álvarez**, Pugliese's first bandoneonist and arranger. After Álvarez's retirement in 2021 and death in March 2023, the orchestra continues under **Ramiro Boero**.

- 12 CDs played in milongas worldwide
- Performed at TangoBA Festival 2024
- 3 new recordings in 2025, album *Sentido Único* (2023)
- Direct link from Pugliese to present day

**Website:** colortango.com.ar

---

### Orquesta El Arranque (1996–present)

**Director:** Ignacio Varchausky
**Style:** Neo-traditional

Varchausky's own ensemble, among the most prominent revival-era orchestras.

- 10+ albums, 300+ cities toured
- Latin Grammy nominated (2004)
- Performed at TangoBA 2024

---

### Orquesta Típica Fernández Fierro (2001–present)

**Style:** Punk-tango cooperative

Born during Argentina's 2001 economic crisis. OTFF draws from Pugliese's tradition but injects aggressive, rebellious energy with expressionist staging. They operate as a cooperative and run their own venue.

- Own venue: **CAFF** (Club Atlético Fernández Fierro), Sánchez de Bustamante 772, Abasto
- Featured in documentary *Orquesta Típica (Tango or Death)* (2005, dir. Nicolás Entel)
- 7+ studio albums including *Destrucción Masiva* (2003), *Mucha Mierda* (2006), *TICS* (2012), *Ahora y Siempre* (2018)
- Weekly Wednesday residency at CAFF
- Hosts annual **FA CAFF** festival for independent tango
- International tours: Europe, Oceania, Latin America

**Website:** fernandezfierro.com

---

### Orquesta Típica Andariega (2009–present)

**Director:** Luigi Coviello (Italian-born contrabassist)
**Style:** Innovative milonguero

Proposes "tango milonguero y bailable" through surprising, deconstructive arrangements of classics that remain danceable.

- 6 albums including *Avanti* (2024), *Santo Milonguero* (2025)
- Collaborations with singers Marisol Martinez and Walter "Chino" Laborde
- Active in Buenos Aires milonga circuit

**Bandcamp:** orquestatipicaandariega.bandcamp.com

---

### Tango Bardo (~2010–present)

**Director:** Lucas Furno (violin)
**Style:** Neo-traditional / Virtuosic instrumental

Known for virtuosic, high-energy instrumentals. In 2024 launched **Disarliana**, an expanded project recreating Di Sarli's sung repertoire with singers Roberto Minondi and Chino Laborde.

- Multiple albums, highly rated by DJs and dancers
- *Disarliana* album (2024) — Di Sarli vocal repertoire
- Regular performers at BA milongas
- International touring via Moonlight Tango

**Bandcamp:** tangobardo.bandcamp.com
**Instagram:** @tango_bardo

---

### Sexteto Fantasma (2012–present)

**Director:** Guido Iacopetti (guitar/composer)
**Style:** Contemporary tango / Fusion

One of the most significant contemporary tango ensembles. Fuses traditional tango with jazz, rock, and Latin American folklore using unconventional instrumentation (trumpet with sordina, jazz guitar).

- **2025 Latin Grammy nomination** for Best Tango Album (*La inevitable tentación de ir a contramano*)
- **Premio Tango Siglo XXI** winner for Best Tango Song
- 4 studio albums: *La Ventanita de Arrabal* (2014), *Fuego Bohemio* (2018), *Noches Milongueras* (2019), *La inevitable tentación* (2024)
- Ran weekly milonga "La Ventanita de Arrabal" for over a decade
- Collaborations with Fito Páez, Paula Maffía, Cucuza Castiello
- Four European tours, 10+ countries

**Current members:** Guido Iacopetti (guitar), Rodrigo Perelsztein (vocals), Ezequiel Agüero (piano), Matías D'Amico (contrabass), Sofía Calvet (bandoneón), Daniel Mayor (trumpet)

**Bandcamp:** sextetofantasma.bandcamp.com
**Instagram:** @sextetofantasma

---

### Romantica Milonguera (~2017–present)

**Director:** Tomás Regolo (piano)
**Style:** Maximally expressive neo-traditional

Known for emotionally intense interpretations. Their style is the polar opposite of Andariega's restraint—Romantica goes "all the way up to the cliff, and then jumps off it."

- Albums: *Romantica Milonguera* (2018), *Boleros* (2020), *Last Tango in Paris* (2024)
- International touring via Moonlight Tango
- Singers: Antonella Alfonso, Marc Bellini

**Bandcamp:** romanticamilonguera.bandcamp.com

## International Orchestras

### Bandonegro (Poland, ~2015–present)

World-class Polish tango orchestra proving tango is now truly international. Their 2025 album *TANUEVO* received acclaim from Argentine media.

- Album *TANUEVO* (2025)
- Own tango festival in Poznań (August 2025)
- Collaboration with symphony orchestras
- Extensive European concert circuit

**Website:** bandonegro.com

---

### Hyperion Ensemble (Italy, 1998–present)

Italian tango ensemble with 9 albums. Performs at major European festivals and Buenos Aires milongas.

- 9 albums (1998–2018)
- Collaborations with singers Marisol Martinez, Antonella Ruggiero
- Active at TangoSud 2025

## The Electronic Branch

### Gotan Project (2001) & Bajofondo (2002)

Electronic tango collectives created music for listeners, not dancers. While controversial among traditionalists, they introduced millions to tango sounds. Both performed live with dancers including Chicho Frúmboli.

## Key Individual Musicians

### Ignacio Varchausky

Perhaps the most important figure in contemporary tango cultural preservation.

- **Bassist**, founder of El Arranque (1996)
- Created **Orquesta Escuela Emilio Balcarce** (2000) — trained ~300 musicians
- Founded **TangoVia Buenos Aires** nonprofit — Digital Tango Archive
- **Latin Grammy** winner as producer (Leopoldo Federico, 2009)
- Author: *The Bass in Tango* (2016), *The Art of Tango* (2021)
- Faculty at Reed College Tango for Musicians
- Festival producer: Buenos Aires Tango Festival in Paris, Rome, Lille, Genoa

## The Ecosystem

### Training Pipeline

The **Orquesta Escuela de Tango Emilio Balcarce** (founded 2000) has trained ~300 musicians who now populate the entire Buenos Aires scene. Currently directed by Víctor Lavallén (previously Néstor Marconi).

### Venues

- **CAFF** (Club Atlético Fernández Fierro) — OTFF's venue, Abasto
- Major milongas feature live orchestra nights

### Festivals

- **TangoBA Festival y Mundial** (Buenos Aires, August) — 500+ activities, 2000+ artists, 40+ venues
- **CITA** (Buenos Aires, March) — Directed by Fabián Salas & Lola Díaz; 2 live orchestras per night
- **FA CAFF** — Annual independent tango festival at CAFF
- **Bandonegro Festival** (Poznań, August)

### The Challenge

Revival orchestras face structural obstacles:
- **The recordings are perfect** — Why hire a live orchestra when you can play Di Sarli?
- **Bandoneón scarcity** — Alfred Arnold factory closed 1948; authentic instruments rare
- **Training rebuilt** — Through Escuela de Música Popular de Avellaneda and Orquesta Escuela

Despite these challenges, the revival has created a living tango music scene that didn't exist in 1985.
